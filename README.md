# Brain-Drain-or-Brain-Gain-
(A pilot study of typing, thinking and remembering.)

Hi everyone! 

I'm doing a small research study called "Typing,Thinking and Retaining". It looks at whether writing with AI helps our thinking or quietly takes over the job, and how much we actually remember afterwards.

It takes about 20 minutes: two short counterarguments (one on your own, one with AI allowed) and a quick memory check. Yes, I know you have a hundred things due, and I promise this one won't ask for a 5,000-word essay.

Your help would mean a lot to me. Your answers are kept confidential and used only for academic research.
